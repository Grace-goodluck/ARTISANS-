About Artisaan's Crib

Nigeria's dedicated marketplace connecting skilled artisans with clients who need them — fast, free, and direct.

Our Mission

Artisaan's Crib was built to solve a simple but frustrating problem: finding a reliable skilled artisan in Nigeria is hard. Word of mouth is unreliable, phone book directories are outdated, and social media makes it difficult to verify who you're hiring.

Our mission is to make it effortless for everyday Nigerians to discover, contact, and hire verified skilled artisans — from tailors and carpenters to plumbers, electricians, makeup artists, and more — all in one trusted place.

Our Vision

To become Nigeria's most trusted directory of skilled tradespeople, empowering artisans to grow their business through digital visibility, and giving clients the confidence to hire quality service without risk or guesswork.

Our Values

Trust

Every artisan registers with real details. Reviews from real clients keep everyone accountable.

Fairness

No commissions, no hidden fees. Artisans keep everything they earn.

Speed

Find and contact an artisan in minutes. No waiting, no middlemen.

Community

We exist to uplift skilled workers and make Nigerian craftsmanship more visible.
